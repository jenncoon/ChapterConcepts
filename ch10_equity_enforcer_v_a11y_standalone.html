The Kingdom's Share Structure

Crestholm Kingdom has two classes of shareholders. The Lords and Ladies hold Preferred Stock: they receive their dividend first, before anyone else. The Commoners hold Common Stock: they vote on kingdom matters and share in the growth, but wait their turn for dividends.

Your job: track exactly how many shares exist, who holds them, and what it all means for the royal equity section.

Treasury Stock note: Treasury Stock is a contra equity account, meaning its balance is subtracted from total equity. When you repurchase shares, the Treasury Stock balance increases (the deduction grows larger), which causes Total Equity to go down. Use UP/DOWN to predict whether the Treasury Stock balance itself changes.

The Five Equity Pillars of Crestholm

  • Common Stock (Par): The par value of shares issued. Usually $0.01, nearly meaningless in modern practice.
  • APIC (Additional Paid-In Capital): The amount received above par when shares are sold.
  • Retained Earnings: Accumulated net income kept in the kingdom, not paid out as dividends.
  • Treasury Stock Balance (contra equity, negative): A running deduction from equity. When this balance increases (UP), the deduction grows and Total Equity falls.
  • Total Equity: What the commoners and lords actually own together.
